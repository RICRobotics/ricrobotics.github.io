RIC Robotics - Est. 2025

RIC Robotics was founded in October of 2025, with the aim of 1) gathering interested students and 2) providing an enviroment to learn about robotics with these like-minded and capable peers. That being said, no experience is required, and in fact the base content is learnable in very little time: all that's needed is interest and effort.

As for specifics, the club meets once week for around two hours. The idea is that these two hours are spent explaining progression in your / your group's area over the last week, and connecting these together. We have some Arduino kits, which are a great way to learn the basics of electrical engineering, programming, and assembling systems for introductory work, and some Raspberry Pis, cameras and motors.
